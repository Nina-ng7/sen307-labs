The reverse-engineered diagram contains the same six tables as my Week 4 model: Department, Lecturer, Student, Course, Enrolment and Teaching. The main difference is that it shows the exact data types and constraints from the real database, such as VARCHAR(100), ENUM(‘M’,’F’), TINYINT and AUTO_INCREMENT primary keys and the last table name was changed to CourseAllocation, whereas my Week 4 diagram only showed general attribute names. It also draws a relationship line for every foreign key, so the many-to-many links between Student and Course (through Enrolment) and between Course and Lecturer (through CourseAllocation) now appear as two one-to-many relationships on each junction table. Finally, the diagram includes the phone column added with ALTER TABLE, which was not in my original design.
